### 👋 About Me

I'm an **AI/ML Engineer and AI Researcher** based in London, building agentic AI systems, computer vision pipelines, and LLM-powered automation for healthcare, finance, and enterprise workflows. I hold an **MSc in Artificial Intelligence Technology (Distinction)** from **Northumbria University, London**, and I work across the full ML lifecycle — from data pipelines and model development to containerised deployment and cloud infrastructure.

- 🔬 Currently researching **multi-modal agentic systems for medical AI** and **graph learning for financial crime detection**
- 🌱 Deepening expertise in **Vision Transformers, Multi-Agent Orchestration, and Weak/Positive-Unlabelled Learning**
- 🤝 Delivered end-to-end AI solutions for international clients across healthcare, automotive due-diligence, and banking compliance
- 📫 Reach me at **malikharis3984@gmail.com**
